Edward King is a writer and teacher based in Colorado. He has been published in the University of Colorado Honors Journal and the Colorado's Emerging Writers series. He edits the journal thousandonestories.com and posts detective stories on coffeebreakreads.com. He strives to write stories that help his readers discover new possibilities for joy in their lives.
